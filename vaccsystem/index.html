About VaxTrack

VaxTrack is a comprehensive vaccine management system designed to streamline the vaccination process for both healthcare providers and patients.

Our platform helps reduce administrative burden, minimize wait times, and ensure timely vaccinations through automated scheduling and reminders.

We work with healthcare facilities nationwide to improve vaccination rates and public health outcomes.
